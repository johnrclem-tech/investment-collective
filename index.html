The Advisor

One person remains accountable for turning complexity into direction.

The Advisor does more than assemble information. The Advisor understands how the family’s investments, structures, obligations, and priorities interact—then makes a clear recommendation and stays responsible for what happens next.

The Council

The right people make difficult decisions easier to examine.

A recurring Council learns the family, remembers prior decisions, and develops enough trust to challenge the attractive story before it becomes conviction. The purpose is not consensus. It is sharper judgment.

Explore The Council →

Honest challenge

Trusted peers can question incentives, concentration, timing, sizing, and assumptions without performing for the room.

Independent decision

The Council improves the reasoning while the family and Advisor retain responsibility for the recommendation and choice.

The Community

When the question is specific, the most relevant experience may sit outside the Council.

The Community makes that experience easier to find without turning other members’ decisions into recommendations. Families can understand who faced a similar question, how they reasoned through it, and what happened afterward.

The Adventure

Shared experiences create the trust that formal meetings cannot manufacture.

Each quarter offers a familiar kind of trip in a new place. Members, Advisors, and Council peers build shared history through experiences designed primarily for conversation, connection, and time together.

Explore The Adventure →
Winter

The Ski Weekend

A different mountain each year, with skiing, fireside dinners, and long stretches of unstructured time together.

Spring

The Sporting Weekend

A different golf course or tennis center and a different skills clinic each year, creating relaxed time to learn and build relationships.

Summer

The Big Cities Weekend

A great city explored through its food, neighborhoods, culture, and one defining live sporting or cultural event.

Fall

The Iconic Experience Weekend

A rotating trip centered on a singular event such as the Kentucky Derby, Formula 1, the Masters, Ryder Cup, or Jazz Fest.
