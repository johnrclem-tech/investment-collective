The relationship

Your first call before an important financial decision.

Your advisor helps identify the real question, assembles the relevant family context, brings in the right specialists and peer experience, and keeps the decision moving after the meeting ends.

What your advisor does

Context. Judgment.
Coordination. Follow-through.

The advisor is responsible for helping the family see the complete decision—not merely the account, product, transaction, or specialist’s portion of it.

01

Understand the complete family

Build and maintain a working understanding of assets, liabilities, trusts, entities, businesses, liquidity, commitments, taxes, goals, and family dynamics.

02

Frame the right decision

Clarify what is actually being decided, which tradeoffs matter, what assumptions are driving the choice, and what could change the answer.

03

Evaluate options independently

Compare alternatives without commissions, proprietary products, placement fees, custody incentives, or compensation tied to transaction volume.

04

Coordinate the right specialists

Bring tax, legal, insurance, banking, custody, and other expertise into the decision while keeping everyone connected to the same family context.

05

Bring in relevant peer experience

Prepare important questions for the peer council and identify useful experience, relationships, or pattern recognition from the broader member community.

06

Drive follow-through

Document decisions, assign next actions, coordinate introductions, track unresolved issues, and revisit whether the original reasoning still holds.

How advice works

Before, during, and after the decision.

Before

Assemble the context

The advisor identifies the decision, gathers the relevant portfolio and family information, tests the assumptions, and determines which specialists or peers should contribute.

During

Make the tradeoffs explicit

The family compares choices, risks, tax and estate implications, liquidity effects, opportunity costs, and relevant experience before deciding.

After

Turn judgment into action

The advisor coordinates implementation, records the reasoning, monitors open items, and schedules the decision for future review.

Independent by design

The advice is the product.

No custody requirementYour assets can remain with existing banks and custodians.
No product economicsNo commissions, proprietary funds, placement fees, or transaction incentives.
No specialist replacementYour advisor coordinates attorneys, CPAs, insurers, lenders, and other experts rather than pretending to replace them.
