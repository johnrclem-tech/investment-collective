Before you apply

This is a fit conversation.
Not a sales funnel.

There is no obligation, product pitch, or transfer requirement. The first step is simply determining whether your needs, expectations, and contribution align with the collective.

Confidential

Your information stays private

The inquiry is used only to evaluate potential membership and prepare for an introductory conversation.

Selective

Fit matters in both directions

Financial complexity matters, but character, curiosity, discretion, and willingness to contribute matter just as much.

Human

No automated acceptance

Every prospective member speaks with a person. Membership is never determined by a score or balance-sheet threshold alone.

The standard

The right member wants better decisions—not more access, activity, or status.

We are looking for experienced investors who value independent thinking, honest reflection, trusted peers, and the discipline to learn from both successes and mistakes.

The process

Deliberate, personal, and mutual.

Step one

Confidential introduction

We review your inquiry and schedule a conversation focused on your financial life, current challenges, expectations, and questions.

Step two

Fit and philosophy

We explore how you make decisions, the advisory relationships you already have, what you hope to gain, and what you would contribute.

Step three

Investment Office assessment

If there appears to be a fit, we outline the initial work required to understand and organize your family capital.

Step four

Council considerations

We consider compatibility, complementary experience, geography, conflicts, and the council environment where you are most likely to thrive.

Step five

A mutual decision

Both sides decide whether to proceed. The goal is not to fill seats quickly; it is to build a collective worth belonging to.
