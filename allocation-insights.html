What it monitors

The portfolio through seven different lenses.

01

Allocation drift

Changes from market movement, commitments, distributions, purchases, sales, and valuation updates.

02

Concentration

Exposure across companies, managers, industries, geographies, entities, and sources of family income.

03

Liquidity

Cash reserves, expected capital calls, debt maturities, taxes, planned purchases, and minimum family reserves.

04

Private-market pacing

Commitment activity, expected distributions, vintage concentration, and the capacity for new investments.

05

Tax exposure

Embedded gains, loss-harvesting opportunities, entity location, income character, and timing considerations.

06

Goal alignment

Whether the portfolio still supports family spending, philanthropy, estate plans, and the next generation.
