Decision 042 · May 2023

Horizon Growth Partners IV

Passed
Proposed commitment$2,000,000
Confidence72%
ReviewedJune 2026
Original thesis

A strong growth-equity manager with differentiated sourcing and credible operating support could compound capital over a ten-year horizon.

Portfolio context

Private equity already represented 18% of Harrison family capital, with $5.9 million of unfunded commitments and limited near-term distributions.

What could prove us wrong

Manager succession, aggressive entry valuations, overlapping technology exposure, and a draw schedule that could tighten liquidity.

Decision

Pass. The fund may be attractive, but the commitment is not right for the family at this point in the portfolio cycle.

Expected outcome

The manager could perform well, while the family would preserve flexibility and keep private-market exposure inside its intended range.

Actual outcome

Early portfolio marks were positive, but distributions remained limited. The preserved liquidity funded a later direct investment with less portfolio overlap.

Lesson carried forward

A strong investment is not automatically the right portfolio decision. Underwrite opportunity quality and family fit separately.
