The Member Experience

Your first year.
One connected system.

Membership begins with understanding, grows through real decisions and trusted relationships, and ends the year with a clearer investment philosophy than the one you started with.

Year one

JANUnderstand everything.
MARMeet your council.
DECDefend your portfolio.
January

Understanding everything

We begin with your entire financial life—not only the assets you may eventually ask us to advise on. Accounts, trusts, private investments, real estate, entities, debt, insurance, advisers, commitments, goals, and expected liquidity are organized into one family balance sheet.

February

Your Investment Office comes to life

You receive a living view of family capital: net worth, liquidity, capital calls, concentrations, major fees, upcoming decisions, and the questions that deserve attention. Clarity comes before advice.

March

Meet your Peer Council

Your council is assembled around judgment, compatibility, confidentiality, and complementary experience—not simply wealth. Six to eight members begin building the trust required for candid conversations.

April

Your first real decision

An opportunity arrives. Instead of wondering whom to call, the process already exists: portfolio context, relevant member experience, council perspective, independent analysis, and a documented decision.

See One Decision
June

Your first quarterly review

The agenda does not begin with market performance. It begins with what changed, what deserves attention, and which decisions are approaching. Performance matters, but decision quality comes first.

August

The network starts working

You ask whether anyone has experience with a manager, strategy, custodian, lender, or adviser. Relevant members and documented lessons appear. The network shortens the distance between a question and useful experience.

October

You become part of the intelligence

A member asks a question you have already faced. Your experience now helps someone else think more clearly. The value of the network begins to compound in both directions.

December

Portfolio Defense

Once a year, every member presents the portfolio, the rationale behind it, the risks that matter, the mistakes made, what changed, and what comes next. No rankings. No performance theater. Just discipline, accountability, and better questions.

Five years later

Your decision history becomes an asset

Your Decision Journal contains the investments evaluated, the opportunities declined, the risks anticipated, the surprises encountered, and the lessons that changed your thinking.

Twenty years later

Your family inherits more than a portfolio

Your children can understand not only what the family owned, but how decisions were made, what principles guided them, and what experience taught you. They inherit judgment—not just statements.

What changes

You stop organizing your financial life around accounts.
You organize it around decisions.

The Investment Office provides context. The council provides perspective. Collective Intelligence provides experience. Independent advice connects it all to your family.
