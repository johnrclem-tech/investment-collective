Not ratings. Decisions.

The reason behind the choice is more useful than the result.

A score tells you whether someone liked something. A documented decision explains what the member was trying to accomplish, why the opportunity fit or did not fit, and what happened afterward.

01What was evaluated?

The opportunity, adviser, structure, or family decision under consideration.

02Why invest, hire, or pass?

The thesis, alternatives, tradeoffs, sizing, and circumstances behind the choice.

03What interests existed?

Ownership, compensation, introductions, and relevant economic relationships.

04What happened later?

Outcomes, surprises, changed assumptions, and whether the member would decide the same way again.

The advisor’s role

Experience informs the recommendation.
It does not become the recommendation.

Your advisor separates useful signal from anecdote, makes conflicts visible, and connects member experience to the complete family picture before recommending what should happen next.

Designed for trust

Private by default.
Useful by permission.

Members control where their experience travels. The community is not a marketplace, a fundraising channel, or a paid referral network.
