The flywheel

Every documented decision makes the network smarter.

01

Evaluate

A member reviews a manager, fund, or direct investment.

02

Document

The thesis, risks, sizing, decision, and conflicts are recorded.

03

Review

The member revisits what happened and what changed.

04

Share

Permissioned experience becomes useful to the next member.

05

Learn

The network identifies patterns across decisions and outcomes.

06

Improve

Members ask better questions and make more deliberate decisions.

Designed for trust

The safeguards are part of the product.

Privacy

Member-controlled sharing

Experience can be shared with a council, selected members, anonymously, or only with the adviser.

Conflicts

Relationships disclosed

Ownership, compensation, introductions, and economic interests travel with the insight.

Culture

Experience, not promotion

No solicitation, no pay-to-play access, and no compensation for member investment activity.
