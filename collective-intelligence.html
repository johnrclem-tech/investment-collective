The gap

The right person often exists.
The family just has no reliable way to find them.

A member may already have evaluated the same manager, worked with the same adviser, negotiated the same transaction, or lived through the same family decision. Useful experience is everywhere—but usually scattered across private relationships and forgotten conversations.

01Experience is fragmented.

The insight sits with individual families, advisers, and personal networks.

02Personal networks are incomplete.

The most relevant person may be outside the family’s immediate circle.

03Lessons disappear.

The reasoning and outcome are rarely preserved after the conversation ends.

How Community works

Search is the front door.
Understanding is the value.

The goal is not to count how many members invested or passed. It is to understand why sophisticated families made different decisions and determine what applies to this family.

01

Ask a specific question

Define the manager, adviser, structure, opportunity, or family decision under consideration.

02

Surface relevant experience

Find documented decisions and members with firsthand experience that matches the question.

03

Understand the reasoning

Examine the thesis, tradeoffs, sizing, conflicts, surprises, outcomes, and what would change the answer.

04

Connect and apply context

Request a permissioned introduction when useful, then connect the experience to the family’s portfolio, liquidity, goals, and priorities.

The advisor’s role

The community broadens the evidence.
Your advisor decides what applies.

Member experience can improve the question, reveal patterns, and surface people worth speaking with. Your advisor remains responsible for separating signal from anecdote and making an independent recommendation for the family.

SignalSeparate recurring patterns from one member’s isolated experience.
ConflictsMake ownership, compensation, introductions, and economic relationships visible.
ContextConnect the experience to allocation, liquidity, taxes, trusts, commitments, and family priorities.
JudgmentMake a clear independent recommendation and coordinate what happens next.

Designed for trust

Private by default.
Useful by permission.

Community works only when members remain in control of how their experience is shared and the network never becomes a marketplace.

Member controlledMembers choose whether experience is identified, anonymous, limited to selected members, or shared only with an advisor.
Permissioned connectionIntroductions and direct conversations happen only with the contributing member’s approval.
Conflicts disclosedOwnership, compensation, referrals, and relevant economic relationships remain attached to the experience.
No sellingNo solicitation, placement fees, pay-to-play visibility, or compensation tied to member investment activity.
