What it manages

The work normally scattered across people, files, and memory.

Portfolio

One family balance sheet

Every major asset and liability is classified by asset type, owner, entity, liquidity, custodian, adviser, tax status, and strategic purpose.

Private markets

Commitments, calls, and manager history

Track subscriptions, capital calls, distributions, valuations, fees, documents, manager interactions, and the original reason each investment was made.

Liquidity

Know what is available before you commit

Forward-looking cash needs, unfunded commitments, debt maturities, tax obligations, planned purchases, and minimum reserves are modeled together.

Risk

See exposure across accounts

Concentrations are measured across custodians, managers, entities, asset classes, geographies, currencies, industries, and underlying private holdings.

Advice

Coordinate the adviser ecosystem

Investment, tax, estate, insurance, banking, and legal advisers work from the same context instead of maintaining separate versions of the family.

Decisions

Preserve the reasoning

Each significant decision records the opportunity, portfolio context, alternatives, risks, peer experience, adviser view, final choice, and later outcome.

The quarterly review

The meeting begins with what changed.
Not a market commentary deck.

Every review is organized around changes in the family, approaching decisions, liquidity, risk, execution, and the lessons emerging from prior choices.

01

What changed?

New assets, distributions, valuations, debt, family needs, tax assumptions, business events, and adviser updates.

02

What needs attention?

Concentrations, underperforming managers, excess cash, document gaps, fee issues, and commitments that no longer fit.

03

What decisions are coming?

New investments, exits, refinancing, gifting, liquidity events, manager changes, and family governance questions.

What it is not

The Investment Office does not require control of your money.

No custody

Your assets stay where they are

The office works across existing custodians, banks, managers, entities, and advisers rather than forcing everything onto one proprietary platform.

No product shelf

Advice is not tied to distribution

There are no commissions, placement fees, proprietary funds, or hidden economics influencing the recommendations you receive.

No performance theater

Clarity before complexity

The system is designed to improve judgment and execution—not overwhelm the family with dashboards, benchmarks, and unnecessary activity.

A better operating system

You should be able to understand your family capital without rebuilding the picture every time a decision arrives.
