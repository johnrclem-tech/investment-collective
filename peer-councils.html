The council’s job

Challenge. Share. Follow through.

01

Challenge assumptions

Members test the thesis, expose blind spots, and separate conviction from familiarity, access, or urgency.

02

Share relevant experience

People who have faced similar managers, structures, or family tradeoffs contribute what they learned—not what they are selling.

03

Create accountability

Open questions, next actions, and review dates are documented so the conversation changes what happens afterward.

The rhythm

Monthly decisions.
Annual perspective.

Regular meetings address live decisions. Once a year, the Annual Allocation Review asks whether every major exposure still deserves its place.

Designed for trust

Candor requires clear rules.

Privacy

Confidential by default

No recordings, screenshots, forwarding, or identification of another member’s information outside the council.

Conflicts

Interests disclosed

Ownership, compensation, introductions, and economic relationships are disclosed before an opportunity is discussed.

Culture

No selling the room

No fundraising, solicitation, placement fees, or compensation tied to another member’s investment activity.

The standard

The strongest council is not the room with the most successful people.
It is the room where successful people can be honest.
