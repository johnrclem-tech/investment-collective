Why The Adventure exists

Trust rarely forms across a conference table.

The most valuable relationships develop through repeated time together: a chairlift conversation, a clinic session, a late dinner after an iconic city event, or a road trip to an unforgettable experience. The Adventure creates the conditions for members and advisors to know one another beyond portfolios and meeting notes.

01

Context becomes personal

Advisors and members learn how one another think, communicate, respond to uncertainty, and make tradeoffs.

02

Candor becomes easier

Informal time lowers the cost of asking a difficult question, challenging an assumption, or admitting uncertainty.

03

Relationships compound

Each gathering adds shared history that makes future conversations faster, more honest, and more useful.

The annual rhythm

Four traditions.
New destinations every year.

The format remains familiar while the location changes. Members know what each Adventure is meant to create, but every year brings a different mountain, resort, city, or iconic event.

Winter

The Ski Weekend

A different mountain destination each year, built around skiing, fireside dinners, and the unstructured conversations that happen between runs.

Aspen · Deer Valley · Jackson Hole · Big Sky
Spring

The Sporting Weekend

Each year brings a different golf course or tennis center and a different skills clinic. Learning, practicing, and playing together creates relaxed time for members, Advisors, and Council peers to build relationships naturally.

Pinehurst · Sea Island · Indian Wells · Scottsdale
Summer

The Big Cities Weekend

A great city experienced through its food, neighborhoods, culture, and one defining live event—baseball, football, tennis, racing, music, or another occasion that belongs to that place.

Boston · Chicago · New York · Miami
Fall

The Iconic Experience Weekend

A rotating trip built around an event people remember for years—from the Kentucky Derby and Formula 1 to the Masters, Ryder Cup, Jazz Fest, or another singular tradition.

Louisville · Austin · Augusta · New Orleans

How this improves investing

Relationships change the quality of the decision.

Time together does not replace analysis. It improves the environment in which analysis is challenged, understood, and acted upon.

01

The Advisor understands more

Informal conversations reveal risk tolerance, family dynamics, communication preferences, ambition, hesitation, and the experiences behind a member’s stated goals.

02

The Council challenges more honestly

Members who trust one another are more willing to question sizing, timing, incentives, concentration, and the assumptions beneath an attractive opportunity.

03

The Community becomes easier to use

Members are more likely to share hard-earned experience, accept an introduction, or offer credible dissent when the network contains real human relationships.

04

Decisions become easier to implement

Clearer context and stronger trust reduce hesitation, misunderstanding, and the friction that often appears after a recommendation is made.
